About ASL Learning Hub

Interactive Learning Platform

A web app for practising the American Sign Language alphabet and numbers, with real-time hand tracking and instant feedback.

Key Features

  • Real-time hand tracking with MediaPipe
  • Gesture scoring against per-sign finger patterns
  • Live confidence meter while you practise
  • Progress tracking and achievements
  • Progress saved locally in your browser

Learning Modes

  • Alphabet (A-Z): all 26 letters of the ASL alphabet
  • Numbers (1-10): the numbers 1 through 10
  • Progress Tracker: your stats and achievements

How to Get Started

  1. Choose a learning mode
  2. Start the camera and position your hand in frame
  3. Record your gesture, then submit it for a score
  4. Track your progress and earn achievements
